ELIXIR Italy has developed a Node-level approach that combines quantitative monitoring, qualitative assessment and communication activities. The work is designed to produce evidence that can be reused for strategic planning, reporting, funding applications and stakeholder engagement.

## Priorities

- Demonstrate the scientific visibility, uptake and reuse of ELIXIR Italy services by analysing publications, acknowledgements, citations and patterns of use across research communities.
- Investigate the innovation potential and broader use of ELIXIR Italy services by identifying and analysing references to these services in patent documents.
- Understand the outcomes and value generated through engagement with industry by collecting and analysing qualitative evidence on companies’ perceptions, experiences, needs, barriers and the changes resulting from their interaction with the Node.
- Combine quantitative evidence on publications, service citations and patents with qualitative impact cases to provide a more complete account of the Node’s scientific, innovation and socio-economic impact.
- Strengthen the visibility of ELIXIR Italy services and impact by making evidence accessible to researchers, companies, funders, policymakers and Node members.

## Action-methodology

- Develop reproducible methodologies and indicators that can be updated over time without creating an excessive monitoring burden.
- Use **REIMPACT, a methodology currently being prepared for publication**, to analyse the scientific visibility, uptake and reuse of individual research infrastructure services.
- Monitor publications acknowledging ELIXIR Italy and scientific publications citing ELIXIR Italy services.
- Analyse patent documents mentioning ELIXIR Italy services to identify evidence of their use in applied research, technological development and innovation-oriented contexts.
- Combine automated searches and text-mining approaches with manual validation to improve the relevance and reliability of the collected evidence.
- Assess industry engagement as qualitative impact through interviews, questionnaires, stakeholder consultations and dedicated engagement activities.
- Use the findings not only to demonstrate impact, but also to improve services, access mechanisms, communication and future engagement with companies.

## Communication/outreach

- Share ELIXIR Italy services, indicators, dashboards, impact stories and supporting evidence through the ELIXIR Italy website, newsletters, social media and other communication channels.
- Use dashboards and visualisations to communicate evidence on publications, service citations, patterns of scientific reuse and patent references.
- Complement quantitative indicators with qualitative industry impact stories, stakeholder perspectives, testimonials and examples of collaboration.
- Adapt and reuse impact evidence for different audiences and purposes, including researchers, companies, funders, policymakers and Node members, through funding applications, institutional reports, strategic documents, standard slide decks, presentations, pitches and engagement activities across ELIXIR.
