Impact pathway logic

Impact pathway logic is one structured way to think about impact, there are many more ways. While there are many approaches, this is how ELIXIR has built impact stories over the years. The pathway is a web of causes and effects that evolves over time, following a logical flow from activities and outcomes to a high-level impact statement.
Status
in development
Primary audience
Head of Nodes, Nodes Coordinators, Impact officers, Communication officers, Technical coordinators, Platform leads, Product managers, Service managers
  1. Understand the impact logic and the importance of building pathways to demonstrate impact
  2. Recognize the value of keeping things simple.
  3. Start applying the logic to your own impact cases.

Start by compiling your resources, activities and outputs

This is the easiest step in the impact pathway logic, as it involves elements within your control or understanding.

What can you list under:

  • Resources (inputs): Funding, skills, staff and other assets.
  • Activities: Initiatives and efforts undertaken using these resources. This is the most creative and time-consuming part of any initiative that you will analyse.
  • Outputs: Immediate, direct results or products attributable to an activity. These are tangible achievements that “cross the finish line.” Not all activities will produce outputs but multiple activities can contribute to a single output.

Best Practice: Group resources, activities and outputs as much as possible to avoid overly detailed lists. To help you group and focus on the most important part of your work, identify what is the problem that you try to solve and what solutions you work on. This will streamline the next steps in the pathway.

Exercise: activities

Which of the following are “activities” in a database impact pathway:

  • Scan for funding opportunities
  • Put together a plan and the objectives of the study
  • Create/maintain a database
  • Prepare a training module on “how to curate data from this database”
  • Meet with other trainers
Answers
  • ✗ Scan for funding opportunities → it is a too early activity and includes the uncertainty of the funding outcome
  • ✗ Put together an implementation study → it is also an early activity, so it can be excluded, especially if you have many other activities, or it could be included if you think this step was very crucial
  • ✔ Create/update a database → this is an everyday work for many scientists
  • ✔ Prepare a training module → this is a crucial and clear activity that will give you an outcome
  • ✔ Meet with other trainers → this is an activity that help you improve your skills in providing training

Exercise: outputs

Which of the following are “outputs” in a database impact pathway:

  • A database is open and user friendly
  • Write a paper on the developed methodology
  • Give the training
  • Advertise the training module
  • Training materials on “how to curate data from this database” is openly available
Answers
  • ✔ A database is user friendly and openly available → this is an immediate outcome of the activity of preparing or improving the methodology for a database to be user friendly and online
  • ✗ Write a paper on the methodology → this is also an activity that has as a tangible outcome the paper (if published), so the methodology is reachable to other researchers
  • ✗ Give the training → this is also an activity that has an outcome that the trainees have received the training or that the training materials are available to the trainees
  • ✗ Advertise a training module → this is an activity that will give as an outcome the module to be findable and public becomes aware of the module
  • ✔ Training materials are openly available → this is an immediate outcome of the activity of preparing and putting a training module online

Identify the most relevant outcomes

Outcomes are the effects that result from stakeholders engaging with or adopting your outputs.

Key differences from previous Steps:

  • While activities and outputs are within your control, outcomes fall into your sphere of influence—they are usually not directly manageable.
  • Outcomes are not one-to-one with outputs. Multiple outputs can contribute to a single outcome, and one output can feed into multiple outcomes.

Exercise: outcomes

Which of the following are “outcomes” in a database impact pathway:

  • Researchers are using and extracting data from the database
  • Research is built on the top of this database
  • Receive feedback from users and trainees
  • Data are interoperable to other databases
  • Trainees have gained new skills in data curation
Answers
  • ✔ Researchers are extracting data from the database → if the research community uses the database, then this is an outcome as it is out of your control if others will use this resource
  • ✔ Research is built on the top of this database → if the database is cited or mentioned in other research, then this is an outcome
  • ✗ Receive feedback from users and trainees → This is an activity that creates a loop, as it is coming after you have done the first round of activities outputs of creating and making the database openly accessible. This activity has as an outcome a more user-friendly and inclusive database and this contributes to the outcome of “researchers are using the database” and “research is built on the top of this database”
  • ✗ Data are interoperable to other databases → This is an output of the activity of standardising the data and connecting the database with other databases. This output then will feed the outcomes of “researchers are using the database” and “research is built on the top of this database”
  • ✔ Trainees have gained new skills in data curation → based on the attendance, the feedback and the usage of the database by the trainees you can make this claim. Again this is out of your control as you are not forcing anyone to join a training

Define impact areas

Impact areas represent the long-lasting changes that stem from outcomes. Impact can occur in science, society and the economy, aligning with national and international priorities. Unlike outcomes and activities, impacts are harder to document, monitor or quantify because they exist in the sphere of interest rather than control or influence.

As you progress along the impact pathway, the list of mapped elements should narrow. This means that many activities and outputs contribute to fewer outcomes and these outcomes lead to even fewer impact areas. However, multiple outcomes can contribute to a single impact area and one outcome can feed into multiple impact areas.

Considering the type of impact pathway you are constructing, the impact areas can vary in complexity. Depending on the evidence you collect, these areas can be high-level or more simplified and speculative. Below are some impact areas identified for ELIXIR—use these as inspiration when defining your own.

Examples of ELIXIR’s impact areas as part of impact pathways

  • Activities related to FAIRness, workflows improvements, ontologies integration and peer-reviewed articles
    • → outcomes could involve resource reuse, projects or AI/ML applications reusing ELIXIR-supported resources
    • = Indicators: Usage evaluation through user numbers, number of citations
    • → Impact areas: knowledge creation, transfer, research efficiency, productivity
    • = indicators: scientific domain of citations, case studies demonstrating improved processes
  • Training activities and communication/dissemination of training modules
    • → outcome is the new skills that the trainees gained and inclusivity
    • = indicators: Analysis of trainees (including numbers, feedback and trainees background) and reuse of training materials
    • → Impact areas: Skills development and efficiency gains
    • = Indicators: Understanding of long-term gains understanding by re-doing feedback analysis and interviews

Exercise: impact areas

Which of the following are “impact areas” in a database impact pathway:

  • Knowledge creation and innovation
  • Competitiveness in new domains and geographical regions
  • Cost savings for companies
  • New funding is provided to maintain the database
  • Better health due to medicine availability
Answers
  • ✔ Knowledge transfer and innovation → This is an impact from the outcomes “researchers are using the database” and “research is built on the top of this database”
  • ✔ Competitiveness in new domains and geographical regions → Research capacity in new domains and geographical regions → This is an impact that you can state if you can show that the database is used beyond the expected community
  • ✔ Cost savings for companies → This is an impact that you can state if you had trainees from companies and/or you have evidence that they have used the database. This will be related to the outcome “trainees have gained new skills in data curation” and/or “ “researchers are using the database”.
  • ✗ New funding is provided to maintain the database → This is an outcome that is based to activities like proposal writing and/or lobbying. The impact of this outcome is a sustainable database
  • ✔ Better health due to medicine availability → If we have a use case showcasing the contributions that ELIXIR databases have on the creation of this medicine. It follows again the outcomes of “researchers are using the database” and “research is built on the top of this database”.

Examples of impact pathway logics

Impact pathways can range from elaborate (e.g. for large projects) to simplified (e.g. activities ➤ outcomes ➤ impacts). The key is to focus on what is strategic and meaningful for the purpose of building the pathway.
